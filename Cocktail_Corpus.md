# Master Cocktail Manual Corpus

---

# Old Fashioned
- **Category:** Classic / Stirred
- **Glassware:** Rocks / Lowball Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Bourbon or Rye Whiskey
- **Flavor Profile:** Spirit-Forward, Rich, Bittersweet
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Bourbon Whiskey, 1 Sugar Cube (or 0.25 oz Simple Syrup), 2 dashes Angostura Bitters, 1 dash Orange Bitters, Splash of water.
- **Preparation:** Muddle sugar, bitters, and water in a rocks glass until sugar dissolves. Add ice cubes and whiskey. Stir gently for 30 seconds. Express orange peel oil over the glass and drop as garnish.
- **Origin/Notes:** Originating in Louisville, Kentucky in the 19th century, it is the quintessential classic whiskey cocktail.

---

# Manhattan
- **Category:** Classic / Stirred
- **Glassware:** Coupe or Nick & Nora Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Rye Whiskey
- **Flavor Profile:** Spirit-Forward, Herbal, Complex
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Rye Whiskey, 1.0 oz Sweet Red Vermouth, 2 dashes Angostura Bitters.
- **Preparation:** Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice. Stir well for 30 seconds until chilled. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a brandied Luxardo cherry.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created at the Manhattan Club in New York City in the 1870s.

---

# Negroni
- **Category:** Classic / Equal Parts / Stirred
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Bitter, Herbal, Citrusy, Rich
- **Ingredients:** 1.0 oz London Dry Gin, 1.0 oz Campari, 1.0 oz Sweet Red Vermouth.
- **Preparation:** Combine all ingredients into a rocks glass filled with a large ice cube. Stir gently until chilled. Garnish with an orange slice or orange peel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Invented in Florence, Italy in 1919 when Count Camillo Negroni asked for gin in his Americano.

---

# Margarita
- **Category:** Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Margarita / Coupette Glass (Salt Rim)
- **Base Spirit:** Blanco Tequila
- **Flavor Profile:** Citrusy, Tart, Salty, Refreshing
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Blanco Tequila, 1.0 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.75 oz Cointreau / Triple Sec, 0.25 oz Agave Nectar (optional).
- **Preparation:** Rub lime wedge around rim of glass and dip in kosher salt. Shake all ingredients vigorously with ice for 15 seconds. Double strain into chilled, salted glass over fresh ice. Garnish with lime wheel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created in Mexico in the 1930s–1940s; one of the world's most popular tequila cocktails.

---

# Daiquiri
- **Category:** Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** White Rum
- **Flavor Profile:** Crisp, Tart, Balanced, Refreshing
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz White Rum, 1.0 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.75 oz Simple Syrup (1:1).
- **Preparation:** Add all ingredients into a shaker filled with ice. Shake vigorously until frost forms on the outside. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a lime wheel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Originating in the Cuban village of Daiquiri around 1898; Ernest Hemingway's favorite style of Cuban cocktail.

---

# Mojito
- **Category:** Highball / Tropical / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Highball / Collins Glass
- **Base Spirit:** White Rum
- **Flavor Profile:** Herbal, Citrusy, Effervescent, Light
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz White Rum, 1.0 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.75 oz Simple Syrup, 6-8 Fresh Mint Leaves, Top with Soda Water.
- **Preparation:** Lightly muddle mint leaves with simple syrup and lime juice in shaker. Add rum and ice, shake briefly. Strain into highball glass over fresh crushed ice. Top with soda water and garnish with a mint sprig.
- **Origin/Notes:** Traditional Cuban highball born in Havana in the early 20th century.

---

# Espresso Martini
- **Category:** Modern Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Coupe or Cocktail Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Vodka
- **Flavor Profile:** Coffee, Rich, Velvety, Sweet
- **Ingredients:** 1.5 oz Vodka, 1.0 oz Freshly Brewed Espresso (cooled), 0.75 oz Kahlúa Coffee Liqueur, 0.25 oz Simple Syrup.
- **Preparation:** Add all ingredients into shaker with ice. Shake hard for 20 seconds to create rich crema foam. Double strain into chilled coupe. Garnish with 3 floating coffee beans.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Dick Bradsell at the Soho Brasserie in London in 1983.

---

# Aperol Spritz
- **Category:** Highball / Aperitif
- **Glassware:** Large Wine Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Aperol Liqueur
- **Flavor Profile:** Bittersweet, Orange, Fizzy, Light
- **Ingredients:** 3.0 oz Prosecco, 2.0 oz Aperol, 1.0 oz Soda Water.
- **Preparation:** Fill a large wine glass with ice. Pour Prosecco first, followed by Aperol and a splash of soda water to prevent Aperol settling at bottom. Garnish with an orange slice and olive.
- **Origin/Notes:** Iconic Italian aperitivo from the Veneto region, popularized in the 1950s.

---

# Mai Tai
- **Category:** Tiki / Tropical / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Double Rocks Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Aged Jamaican Rum & Martinique Rum
- **Flavor Profile:** Nutty, Citrusy, Complex, Boozy
- **Ingredients:** 1.0 oz Aged Jamaican Rum, 1.0 oz Martinique Rum, 0.75 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.5 oz Pierre Ferrand Dry Curaçao, 0.5 oz Orgeat (Almond Syrup), 0.25 oz Rich Simple Syrup.
- **Preparation:** Shake all ingredients with crushed ice. Pour unstrained into double rocks glass. Float spent lime shell and garnish lavishly with a fresh mint sprig.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Victor "Trader Vic" Bergeron in Oakland, California in 1944.

---

# Penicillin
- **Category:** Modern Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Blended Scotch & Peated Islay Scotch
- **Flavor Profile:** Smoky, Honey, Ginger, Citrus
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Blended Scotch Whiskey, 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice, 0.75 oz Honey-Ginger Syrup, 0.25 oz Peated Islay Scotch (float).
- **Preparation:** Shake blended scotch, lemon juice, and honey-ginger syrup with ice. Strain into rocks glass over large ice cube. Gently float peated Islay scotch over back of spoon. Garnish with candied ginger.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Sam Ross at Milk & Honey in New York City in 2005.

---

# Sazerac
- **Category:** Classic / Stirred
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass (No Ice)
- **Base Spirit:** Rye Whiskey or Cognac
- **Flavor Profile:** Anise, Spirit-Forward, Herbal, Warm
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Rye Whiskey, 1 Cube White Sugar, 3 dashes Peychaud's Bitters, 1 dash Angostura Bitters, 0.25 oz Herbsaint or Absinthe (rinse).
- **Preparation:** Rinse chilled rocks glass with absinthe and discard excess. In mixing glass, muddle sugar cube with bitters and splash of water. Add rye and ice, stir well. Strain into absinthe-rinsed glass without ice. Express lemon peel oil and discard peel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Official cocktail of New Orleans, created in the mid-19th century.

---

# Last Word
- **Category:** Equal Parts / Pre-Prohibition / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Herbal, Tart, Piquant, Sweet
- **Ingredients:** 0.75 oz London Dry Gin, 0.75 oz Green Chartreuse, 0.75 oz Luxardo Maraschino Liqueur, 0.75 oz Fresh Lime Juice.
- **Preparation:** Combine equal parts of all four ingredients into a shaker with ice. Shake vigorously until thoroughly chilled. Fine strain into a coupe glass. Garnish with a brandied cherry.
- **Origin/Notes:** Invented in the 1920s at the Detroit Athletic Club; revived by Murray Stenson in Seattle in 2004.

---

# Paloma
- **Category:** Highball / Refreshing
- **Glassware:** Highball Glass (Salt Rim)
- **Base Spirit:** Reposado or Blanco Tequila
- **Flavor Profile:** Grapefruit, Fizzy, Tart, Salty
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Reposado Tequila, 0.5 oz Fresh Lime Juice, Pinch of Salt, Top with Grapefruit Soda (e.g. Jarritos or Squirt).
- **Preparation:** Rim highball glass with salt and fill with ice. Add tequila, lime juice, and salt. Top with grapefruit soda and stir gently. Garnish with a grapefruit wedge.
- **Origin/Notes:** The most beloved tequila highball in Mexico, created by Don Javier Delgado Corona in Tequila, Mexico.

---

# French 75
- **Category:** Classic / Champagne Cocktail
- **Glassware:** Champagne Flute
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin or Cognac
- **Flavor Profile:** Sparkling, Botanical, Bright, Citrusy
- **Ingredients:** 1.5 oz London Dry Gin, 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice, 0.5 oz Simple Syrup, 3.0 oz Chilled Champagne.
- **Preparation:** Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice. Strain into a champagne flute. Top with chilled Champagne. Garnish with a long lemon twist.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created during WWI at the New York Bar in Paris; named after the powerful French 75mm field gun.

---

# Boulevardier
- **Category:** Classic / Stirred
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Bourbon or Rye Whiskey
- **Flavor Profile:** Rich, Bitter, Warming, Oaky
- **Ingredients:** 1.5 oz Bourbon Whiskey, 1.0 oz Campari, 1.0 oz Sweet Red Vermouth.
- **Preparation:** Combine all ingredients into a mixing glass with ice. Stir until thoroughly chilled (30 seconds). Strain into rocks glass over a large ice cube. Garnish with an orange peel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Erskine Gwynne, an American writer in Paris, in the 1920s; essentially a whiskey Negroni.

---

# Gimlet
- **Category:** Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Tart, Citrusy, Botanical, Crisp
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz London Dry Gin, 0.75 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.75 oz Simple Syrup.
- **Preparation:** Combine gin, lime juice, and simple syrup in a shaker filled with ice. Shake vigorously for 15 seconds. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a lime wheel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Originally served to Royal Navy sailors to prevent scurvy, using Rose's Lime Juice Cordial.

---

# Moscow Mule
- **Category:** Highball / Buck
- **Glassware:** Copper Mug
- **Base Spirit:** Vodka
- **Flavor Profile:** Spicy, Ginger, Citrus, Effervescent
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Vodka, 0.5 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 4.0 oz Spicy Ginger Beer.
- **Preparation:** Squeeze lime juice into a copper mug and drop spent lime shell in. Fill mug with ice, pour vodka, and top with ginger beer. Stir gently. Garnish with a mint sprig and lime wheel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created in Los Angeles in 1941 to market Smirnoff vodka and Cock 'n Bull ginger beer.

---

# Cosmopolitan
- **Category:** Modern Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Cocktail / Martini Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Citrus Vodka
- **Flavor Profile:** Tart, Fruit-Forward, Crisp
- **Ingredients:** 1.5 oz Citrus Vodka, 0.75 oz Cointreau, 0.5 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.5 oz Cranberry Juice.
- **Preparation:** Add all ingredients into a shaker with ice. Shake hard for 15 seconds. Double strain into a chilled martini glass. Flame an orange peel over top and garnish.
- **Origin/Notes:** Popularized by Toby Cecchini in New York City in 1988 and famous worldwide via *Sex and the City*.

---

# Tom Collins
- **Category:** Highball / Fizz
- **Glassware:** Collins Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Old Tom Gin or London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Citrus, Fizzy, Light, Botanical
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Old Tom Gin, 1.0 oz Fresh Lemon Juice, 0.75 oz Simple Syrup, Top with Soda Water.
- **Preparation:** Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice. Strain into a Collins glass filled with fresh ice. Top with soda water and stir gently. Garnish with a lemon wheel and maraschino cherry.
- **Origin/Notes:** Memorialized in Jerry Thomas's 1876 bartender guide following the Great Tom Collins Hoax of 1874.

---

# Clover Club
- **Category:** Pre-Prohibition / Shaken (Egg White)
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Raspberry, Creamy, Tart, Silky
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz London Dry Gin, 0.5 oz Raspberry Syrup, 0.5 oz Fresh Lemon Juice, 0.5 oz Fresh Egg White (or Aquafaba).
- **Preparation:** Dry shake all ingredients without ice for 15 seconds to emulsify egg white. Add ice and wet shake hard for 15 seconds. Double strain into chilled coupe. Garnish with 3 speared raspberries.
- **Origin/Notes:** Named after the Bellevue-Stratford Philadelphia gentlemen's club in the 1890s.

---

# Bramble
- **Category:** Modern Classic / Crushed Ice
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Blackberry, Citrusy, Refreshing
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz London Dry Gin, 1.0 oz Fresh Lemon Juice, 0.5 oz Simple Syrup, 0.5 oz Crème de Mûre (Blackberry Liqueur).
- **Preparation:** Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice. Strain into rocks glass filled with crushed ice. Drizzle Crème de Mûre over the crushed ice to create a bleeding effect. Garnish with fresh blackberries and lemon slice.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Dick Bradsell at Fred's Club in Soho, London in 1984.

---

# Dark 'n' Stormy
- **Category:** Highball / Buck
- **Glassware:** Highball Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Gosling's Black Seal Rum
- **Flavor Profile:** Rich Molasses, Spicy Ginger, Tart
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Gosling's Black Seal Rum, 0.5 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 4.0 oz Spicy Ginger Beer.
- **Preparation:** Fill highball glass with ice, add lime juice and ginger beer. Gently float dark rum on top using back of spoon. Serve layered with a lime wheel garnish.
- **Origin/Notes:** Trademarked by Gosling Brothers Ltd in Bermuda, created after WWI.

---

# Aviation
- **Category:** Pre-Prohibition / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** London Dry Gin
- **Flavor Profile:** Floral, Botanical, Tart, Subtle Sweetness
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz London Dry Gin, 0.5 oz Maraschino Liqueur, 0.25 oz Crème de Violette, 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice.
- **Preparation:** Combine all ingredients into shaker with ice. Shake vigorously until chilled. Double strain into chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a brandied Luxardo cherry.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Hugo Ensslin at the Hotel Wallick in New York City in 1916; known for its pale sky-blue hue.

---

# Sidecar
- **Category:** Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass (Sugar Rim)
- **Base Spirit:** Cognac
- **Flavor Profile:** Rich Grape, Orange Citrus, Warm, Crisp
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Cognac, 0.75 oz Cointreau, 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice.
- **Preparation:** Coat rim of coupe glass with white sugar. Shake cognac, Cointreau, and lemon juice with ice for 15 seconds. Double strain into prepared coupe. Garnish with a lemon orange twist.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created around the end of WWI at either Harry's New York Bar in Paris or the Ritz Hotel.

# Mint Julep
- **Category:** Classic / Crushed Ice / Muddled
- **Glassware:** Silver or Pewter Julep Cup
- **Base Spirit:** Bourbon Whiskey
- **Flavor Profile:** Mint, Oaky Bourbon, Cold, Refreshing
- **Ingredients:** 2.5 oz Bourbon Whiskey, 8-10 Fresh Mint Leaves, 0.5 oz Simple Syrup (or 1 tsp Powdered Sugar).
- **Preparation:** Gently muddle mint and syrup in Julep cup. Pack cup halfway with crushed ice and add bourbon. Stir until glass frosts. Heap additional crushed ice to form a snow cone. Garnish generously with mint bouquet.
- **Origin/Notes:** The official drink of the Kentucky Derby since 1938.

---

# Pisco Sour
- **Category:** Classic / Shaken (Egg White)
- **Glassware:** Rocks or Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Peruvian Pisco
- **Flavor Profile:** Earthy Grape, Citrus, Creamy, Velvety
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Peruvian Pisco, 1.0 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.75 oz Simple Syrup, 1 Fresh Egg White, 3 drops Amargo Chuncho (or Angostura) Bitters.
- **Preparation:** Dry shake pisco, lime juice, simple syrup, and egg white without ice for 15 seconds. Add ice and wet shake vigorously. Strain into glass. Drop 3 drops of bitters onto foam surface.
- **Origin/Notes:** Invented by American bartender Victor Morris at Morris' Bar in Lima, Peru in the 1920s.

---

# Jungle Bird
- **Category:** Tiki / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Black Strap / Dark Jamaican Rum
- **Flavor Profile:** Bittersweet, Tropical Pineapple, Molasses
- **Ingredients:** 1.5 oz Black Strap Rum, 0.75 oz Campari, 1.5 oz Fresh Pineapple Juice, 0.5 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.5 oz Demerara Simple Syrup.
- **Preparation:** Add all ingredients to shaker with ice. Shake hard for 15 seconds to create pineapple foam. Strain over fresh ice into rocks glass. Garnish with pineapple wedge and leaf.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created at the Aviary Bar inside the Kuala Lumpur Hilton in Malaysia in 1973.

---

# Paper Plane
- **Category:** Equal Parts / Modern Classic
- **Glassware:** Coupe Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Bourbon Whiskey
- **Flavor Profile:** Amaro, Citrus, Bittersweet, Perfectly Balanced
- **Ingredients:** 0.75 oz Bourbon Whiskey, 0.75 oz Amaro Nonino Quintessentia, 0.75 oz Aperol, 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice.
- **Preparation:** Combine equal parts of all four ingredients into a shaker with ice. Shake hard for 15 seconds. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a miniature paper airplane (optional).
- **Origin/Notes:** Created by Sam Ross at the Violet Hour in Chicago in 2008; named after the M.I.A. song *Paper Planes*.

# Mezcalita (Spicy Mezcal Margarita)
- **Category:** Modern Classic / Shaken
- **Glassware:** Rocks Glass (Tajín Rim)
- **Base Spirit:** Mezcal
- **Flavor Profile:** Smoky, Spicy, Citrusy, Complex
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Artisanal Mezcal, 1.0 oz Fresh Lime Juice, 0.75 oz Cointreau, 0.5 oz Agave Nectar, 2 slices Fresh Jalapeño.
- **Preparation:** Rim rocks glass with Tajín chili-lime salt. Lightly muddle jalapeño slices in shaker. Add mezcal, lime juice, Cointreau, agave nectar, and ice. Shake hard for 15 seconds. Strain over large ice cube. Garnish with jalapeño wheel.
- **Origin/Notes:** A smoky, spicy Oaxacan twist on the traditional tequila Margarita.

# Screwdriver
- **Category:** Classic / Highball
- **Glassware:** Highball Glass
- **Base Spirit:** Vodka
- **Flavor Profile:** Citrusy, Bright, Simple, Refreshing
- **Ingredients:** 2.0 oz Vodka, 4.0 oz Fresh Orange Juice.
- **Preparation:** Fill a highball glass with ice. Add vodka and top with fresh orange juice. Stir gently to combine. Garnish with an orange wheel.
- **Origin/Notes:** Created in the 1940s by American oil workers in the Persian Gulf who stirred vodka and orange juice with their screwdrivers.
